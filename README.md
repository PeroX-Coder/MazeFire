# 🔥 MazeFire

**One maze. Five levels. One timer. No second chances.**

MazeFire is a free-to-play 3D maze completion game where every second counts. Navigate through challenging mazes, find your way to the finish, and survive all five levels as the difficulty increases.

But there's a catch: **Run out of time, and you're sent straight back to Level 1.**

No checkpoints. No mercy. Just you, the maze, and the clock.

## 🎮 Game Overview

- **Genre:** 3D Maze / Puzzle / Challenge
- **Levels:** 5 progressively challenging levels
- **Platform:** Web Browser
- **Price:** Free to Play
- **Objective:** Complete all 5 levels without running out of time.

## ⚡ Key Features

- 🧩 **Progressive Difficulty:** Every level introduces a greater challenge.
- ⏱️ **Time Pressure:** Each level comes with its own countdown timer.
- 🔄 **Full Restart Mechanic:** Fail to finish before time runs out? Back to Level 1.
- 🌐 **Browser-Based:** Play directly online without installing anything.
- 🔥 **Rage-Inducing Gameplay:** Every wrong turn costs valuable time.

## 🏁 How to Play

1. Open MazeFire in your browser.
2. Start your journey through Level 1.
3. Navigate the maze and reach the finish before the timer expires.
4. Advance through increasingly difficult levels.
5. Complete all five levels to conquer MazeFire.

**Remember:** The timer doesn't wait for you.
